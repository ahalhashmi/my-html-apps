Eye Draw 1.0 · Use a stand, even front lighting, and a comfortable viewing distance. Take breaks from sustained winking.

Run as a real HTTPS webpage

Upload this HTML file to an HTTPS website and open that page directly in Safari. A Files preview, chat attachment preview, or an HTTP address on your local network is not the intended camera environment. This file deliberately blocks camera startup on file URLs and insecure origins.

Allow the front camera

Tap Start camera, then Allow. If permission was denied, change the website's Camera permission in Safari and reload. A preview embedded in another site may be blocked; open the page by itself.

Calibrate in your drawing position

Place the device on a stand. Keep your head still, open both eyes normally, and look directly at each dot, not at the camera. Calibration learns each eye separately, then checks three additional targets. Recalibrate after changing orientation, distance, or posture.

Draw with one eye

The default is your LEFT eye closed and your RIGHT eye looking around. Open both to stop. Closing both, losing the face, or hiding the page releases the pen. Brief ordinary two-eye blinks are rejected. A quick, deliberate single-eye wink can make a dot.

When aim drifts

Use Recenter aim for a small offset. Use Refine aim while winking if the pointer shifts when an eye closes. More smoothing reduces jitter but adds delay. Eyeglass reflections, small eye images, squinting, head movement, and lighting can reduce usability.

What this prototype can and cannot do

This is calibrated estimation from iris landmarks, not a hardware-grade gaze tracker. It controls a pointer only inside this drawing area, not the iPad/iPhone system cursor. Setup and toolbar controls still use touch. Accuracy needs testing with your own eyes and camera.

Single file, online engine

All app code, styles, calibration, and drawing logic are here. On Start, this page downloads Google's MediaPipe JavaScript/WASM engine from jsDelivr (or unpkg as a fallback) and its face model from Google storage. No API key or processing server is used. Network access is needed; this is not a bundled offline app.

Privacy

This app passes camera frames to the model locally in the browser and does not contain a frame-upload, recording, analytics, or identity-recognition feature. Dependency downloads still contact those third-party hosts. Only your tuning settings are stored locally; gaze calibration, landmarks, and strokes are held in memory. Stop camera ends the media tracks.

Saving your drawing

Save opens a PNG preview. Use Share / Save where supported, Download PNG, or touch and hold the preview image. The exported picture has a plain white background without the pointer, grid, or camera preview. Leaving or reloading the page discards unsaved strokes.

On iPhone / iPad, you can also touch and hold the image to save it.
