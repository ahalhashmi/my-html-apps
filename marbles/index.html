Vertical Tube Physics

Gravity always pulls the marble downward. A vertical phone tap or shake gives it an inertial bounce, letting it strike the top and bottom caps. Strong repeated impacts crack and eventually break the glass.

Tap the lower part of the screen to launch the marble upward. Drag quickly to imitate phone shaking. Desktop: arrows steer, Space runs a bounce demo, and R resets.
