How it works

Reach the goal by changing one letter at a time. Every submitted step must be a valid 4-letter English word.

  2. Do not rearrange the letters.
  3. Every step must exist in the built-in dictionary.
  4. Lower moves are better; invalid attempts and hints reduce score.
  5. Every generated puzzle is verified to have a solution.
